---
title: "Purchase"
description: "Issuer-directed bank-transfer purchase intents are retired."
---

Issuer-directed bank-transfer purchase intents are no longer available.

Primary purchase for investors is from the Juggervault account balance in the application. Partner API routes that create or advance bank-transfer intents return `410` with:

`Issuer-directed bank allocation is no longer available. Purchase from your Juggervault account balance.`

List and cancel routes for existing intents remain available for cleanup. See [Accounts and custody](/guides/accounts-and-custody) and [Fees](/guides/fees).
