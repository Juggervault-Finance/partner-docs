## Partner USDC purchase intents

After registration is complete (`whitelisted`), create a Partner purchase intent:

`POST /assets/{assetId}/purchase-intents`

The investor's Juggervault account must be open. If it is not, the response is `409` and `error` is `The investor's Juggervault account must be set up before creating a purchase intent.`

The response includes the intent and `paymentInstructions` with:

- `receiveAddress`: the investor's own Juggervault account address. The investor sends the USDC there.
- Exact `amount` and `tokenAmount`. `amount` includes the 1% execution fee: the units multiplied by the unit price, plus 1%. See [Fees](/guides/fees).
- `paymentReference`
- `chainId`

Example: 5 units at 10 USDC is 50 USDC for the shares plus 0.5 USDC execution fee, so `amount` is `50.5`.

`bankName`, `accountName`, and `accountNumber` are always `null` on this path. Use the same fields on `GET /purchase-intents/{intentId}` and `GET /investors/{externalUserId}/intents`.

## Settlement

After the investor sends the USDC, record the transfer hash with `POST /purchase-intents/{intentId}/payment`. `bankReference` must be the transaction hash (`0x` followed by 64 hexadecimal characters). A hash already recorded on another intent returns `409`. The intent moves to `payment_submitted`.

Juggervault then settles the purchase without further calls:

1. It reads the transfer and confirms that it paid at least `amount` in USDC into the investor's account, and that the account still holds that amount.
2. The investor's account buys the units through the primary sale. Units are issued to that account.
3. The intent moves to `settled`. `purchase.settled`, which carries the `intentId`, and `holding.updated` are sent. See [Webhooks](/guides/webhooks).

Juggervault pays the processing fee for both settlement transactions. It is never deducted from the investor's USDC, so the full `amount` goes to the purchase. If the processing fee cannot be covered, nothing is submitted, the USDC stays in the investor's account, and settlement is retried.

| Outcome | Intent status |
| --- | --- |
| Transfer not yet readable on the network | Stays `payment_submitted`. Settlement is retried every few minutes. |
| Transfer below `amount`, sent to another address, or failed | Returns to `pending_payment`. Record a new transfer hash. |
| Purchase could not be completed | Stays `payment_submitted`. The USDC stays in the investor's account and settlement is retried. |
| Units issued | `settled` |

The House API does not settle Partner USDC intents. `POST /house/offerings/{offeringId}/intents/{intentId}/settle` returns `410`.

Requires `purchase:write`. The investor must be linked to the calling broker.

## Issuer-directed bank allocation (retired)

Issuer-directed bank-transfer purchase intents are no longer available on the bank path. Bank create and settle routes return `410` with:

`Issuer-directed bank allocation is no longer available. Purchase from your Juggervault account balance.`

Primary purchase in the hosted application remains from the Juggervault account balance. See [Accounts and custody](/guides/accounts-and-custody).
