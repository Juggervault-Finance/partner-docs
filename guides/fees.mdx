---
title: "Fees"
description: "Platform execution fee on a primary purchase, and the broker share of that fee."
---

Each completed primary purchase carries a platform execution fee charged on top of the share price. The standard rate is 1% of the share consideration. The rate in force is the rate configured for that environment. Confirm it on the broker agreement.

If the share consideration is 10,000, the investor pays 10,100. The issuer receives the full consideration. The 100 is the platform fee.

## Fee allocation

| Origin | Broker share | Juggervault retains |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Partner API, or the investor's first purchase from a referral | 0.7% of the purchase, invoiced outside the platform | 0.3% |
| Direct purchase with no broker origin | None | The full 1% |

`POST /assets/{assetId}/purchase-intents` records purchase channel `partner_api` and the broker identifier on the intent and on the settled sale. That record marks the purchase as broker-originated.

The standard broker share is 70% of the 1% fee, which is 0.7% of the share consideration. Where a different broker share is agreed, the percentage configured for that environment applies.

## Separate charges

Placement fees, ordinarily 2% to 4% of capital raised, are invoiced offline to the asset owner. They are separate from the execution fee.

The Partner API purchase intent is a bank-transfer instruction. The amount to pay is the consideration plus the execution fee. Account-balance purchases made by the investor after sign-in follow the same fee, collected with the payment.
