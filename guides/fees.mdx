---
title: "Fees"
description: "Platform execution fee on a primary purchase, and the broker share of that fee."
---

Each completed primary purchase carries a platform execution fee charged on top of the share price. The rate is set for each offering before its primary sale opens, from the offering's stated asset value:

| Stated asset value | Execution fee |
| --- | --- |
| Up to $5 million | 1.00% of the share consideration |
| Above $5 million | 0.75% of the share consideration |
| Above $20 million | 0.50% of the share consideration |

The catalog returns the rate for each offering as `executionFeeRate`, a fraction of the share consideration (for example, `0.0075` is 0.75%). The issuer receives the full share consideration. Placement is not charged.

At 1%, a share consideration of 10,000 means the investor pays 10,100, and the 100 is the execution fee. At 0.75%, the investor pays 10,075.

## Broker share

The broker share of the execution fee is agreed with each broker, and may be set for a specific offering. Confirm the share in your broker agreement. Juggervault retains the rest of the fee.

| Origin | Broker share |
| --- | --- |
| Partner API purchase | The agreed share of that purchase's execution fee |
| Completed purchase by an investor the broker referred or linked, when it is not a Partner API purchase | The agreed share of that purchase's execution fee |
| Direct purchase with no broker origin | None. Juggervault retains the full fee. |

`POST /assets/{assetId}/purchase-intents` records purchase channel `partner_api` and the broker identifier on the intent and on the settled sale. Every settled purchase by an investor the broker referred or linked carries the broker share. Where partner routing and a referral are both present, partner routing takes precedence.

The broker share is paid to the broker's commission payout account in settlement of each purchase. Where an offering holds payments until its minimum raise is met, the share is paid when the sale is finalized as successful, and none is paid if the sale fails and investors are refunded.

## Amount on a purchase intent

On a Partner API purchase intent, `paymentInstructions.amount` includes the offering's execution fee: the units multiplied by the unit price, plus the fee. For example, 5 units at 10 USDC on an offering at 1% is 50 USDC for the shares plus 0.5 USDC execution fee, so the investor pays 50.5 USDC. On an offering at 0.75%, the same purchase is 50.375 USDC. Account-balance purchases made by the investor after sign-in also collect the fee with the payment.

Juggervault pays the processing fee for Partner settlement transactions. It is not taken from the investor's USDC.
