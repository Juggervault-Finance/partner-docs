---
title: "Investor account"
description: "How a broker-linked investor becomes a Juggervault account that can register and purchase."
---

`POST /investors/link` creates a Juggervault user profile when the email is new, or attaches `externalUserId` to an existing user with that email. The profile may have no password until the investor sets one. Linking does not open the platform account.

Linking does not verify identity and does not register the investor for an offering.

## Investor actions after linking

<Steps>
  <Step title="Identity verification">
    The investor opens `continueUrl`, which points to identity verification until Juggervault approves the record. A prior `kyc-import` supplies the data for that form. It does not approve the record. On that same page the investor completes investor eligibility and self-certifies as accredited. Both are required before registration. The Partner API cannot submit them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Password and sign-in">
    A new profile has no password. Before registration, the investor uses Forgot password with the linked email, sets a password, and signs in. Sign-in opens the platform account and marks the email verified. Registration returns `Complete account setup in Juggervault before requesting registration.` until that account exists.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Registration">
    After approval and sign-in, the partner may call `POST /assets/{assetId}/registration`, or the investor may register in the application. The offering operator must complete registration before purchase. The status is then `whitelisted`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## One identity per account

NIN, BVN, passport number, driver's licence number, voter's card number, and Tax Identification Number are unique on the platform. If a later verification uses an identity already stored under another email, the API returns `409` and `IDENTITY_OWNED_BY_EXISTING_ACCOUNT`. A second profile is not created. The investor signs in with the email that already holds the identity.

## Where units are held

Purchased units are recorded on the investor's Juggervault account. Custody is full platform custody: Juggervault holds the controlling key. Linking does not open the account. After identity verification is approved, the investor signs in and Juggervault opens the account. See [Accounts and custody](/guides/accounts-and-custody).
