---
title: "Sale"
description: "Primary sale, the execution fee, and buyback on JuggervaultSale."
---

`JuggervaultSale` keeps one sale record per offering. The execution fee is 100 basis points, added on top of the list price. See [Fees](/guides/fees).

The buyer permits the total. The sale pulls that amount, sends the list-price amount to the offer operator, sends the fee to the platform fee wallet, and mints the units in the same action. There is no pre-minted pool in the sale contract.

Cash amount equals units times price, divided by 10 to the 18. A minimum purchase that would round that amount to zero is rejected when the sale is opened. A start or end of zero does not impose that bound. A non-zero end must be after the start.

## Primary sale

| Function | Who may call | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `listTokenForSale` | Issuer, or the platform relayer with the issuer signature | Opens the sale. A factory-admin signature is also required. Refuses the call if a sale record already exists. |
| `buyTokens` | Buyer, or the platform relayer for that buyer | Checks the window, registration, and the per-account cap. Pulls cash, then mints. |
| `updateSalePrice` | Issuer, or the platform relayer with the issuer signature | Changes the list price. |
| `endSale` | Issuer, or the platform relayer with the issuer signature | Closes the primary sale. Does not clear the buyback price. |

`buyTokens` refuses the purchase when the account is not registered, the sale is inactive, the window is closed, the remaining capacity is insufficient, or the purchase is outside the minimum, the maximum, or the per-account total.

## Buyback

Buyback does not require the primary sale to remain open. It does require a buyback price above zero.

| Function | Who may call | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `requestSell` | Registered holder | Opens a pending buyback for units the holder already has. |
| `executeBuyback` | Offer operator | Pulls cash from the operator to the holder, and units from the holder to the operator. |
| `cancelBuyback` | Holder or offer operator | Cancels a pending request. No cash or units move. |
| `resetBuybackPrice` | Offer operator, plus a factory-admin signature | Sets a new buyback price. Zero stops new requests. Pending requests can still settle. |

## Partner purchases

A purchase opened through the Partner API is an issuer-directed bank transfer. Settlement of that intent credits the investor's portfolio record. It does not call `buyTokens` and does not mint. Dividends and buyback follow units minted to the platform account. Read both results with the holdings route. That response contains units and share of the project. It does not contain an account address.
