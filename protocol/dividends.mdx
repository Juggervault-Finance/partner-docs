---
title: "Dividends"
description: "How a cash dividend is funded, assigned, and claimed."
---

`JuggervaultDividendDistributor` serves every offering. The settlement currency is the currency of that offering's sale. Cash is reserved on the asset token. Only the distributor can release it.

When units move, the asset token notifies the distributor. A holder's claim tracks those changes. The sale contract and the asset token itself do not share in the dividend. The offer operator may also exclude an account. Excluded units are left out of the eligible supply.

| Function | Who may call | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `distribute` | Issuer of that offering | Pulls settlement currency from the issuer, reserves it on the asset token, and assigns it across eligible supply. |
| `distributeWithPermit` | Issuer, or the platform relayer with the issuer permit | Same assignment. The issuer signs the permit. The relayer may submit it. |
| `claim` | Holder | Pays the caller's claim from the reserve. |
| `claimFor` | Platform relayer only | Pays a named account its own claim. The account receives the cash. |
| `setExcluded` | Issuer, or the platform relayer with the issuer signature | Removes an account, and its units, from the eligible supply. |
| `onTokenTransfer` | Asset token only | Updates dividend records when units move, are minted, or are burned. |

A distribution with no eligible supply is refused. A claim of zero is refused. The amount reserved is the amount actually received, so a fee taken by the settlement currency cannot increase claims.
