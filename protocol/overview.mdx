---
title: "Protocol"
description: "How Juggervault issues project units, settles a primary purchase, and records dividends and buyback."
---

Juggervault issues project units on a permissioned primary sale. The platform decides who may invest. An investor receives units only after identity verification, an open platform account, and completed registration for that offering.

The Partner API does not call the issuance contracts, and it does not accept or return an account address. A partner records a bank-transfer purchase intent. See [Accounts and custody](/guides/accounts-and-custody) and [Purchase](/flows/purchase).

## Life of an offering

<Steps>
  <Step title="Launch project shares">
    Juggervault launches the offering and records the offer operator.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the primary sale">
    The offer operator sets the price, settlement currency, window, and purchase limits. Opening the sale does not issue units.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the account">
    After identity verification, the investor signs in and Juggervault opens the platform account. The investor does not receive a key. See [Accounts and custody](/guides/accounts-and-custody).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete registration">
    The offer operator completes registration for that investor on that offering. Until this step is done, the investor cannot purchase.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Purchase">
    An account-balance purchase issues units to the platform account. The investor pays the share price plus the execution fee. See [Fees](/guides/fees). A Partner API bank transfer is recorded on the portfolio record and does not issue units. See [Purchase](/flows/purchase).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Dividend and buyback">
    Dividends and buyback, where the offering provides them, follow units issued to the platform account. They do not follow a bank-settled portfolio credit.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What must already be true

| Action | Required first |
| --- | --- |
| Registration | Identity verification is approved, and the platform account is open. |
| Purchase | Registration for that offering is complete. |
| Account-balance purchase | The investor pays the share price plus the execution fee from the platform account. |
| Dividend or buyback | The units were issued to the platform account. |
